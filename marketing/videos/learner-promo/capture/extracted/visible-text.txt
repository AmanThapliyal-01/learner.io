Every roadmap online assumes you're starting from zero. learner.io checks where you actually stand, then builds a plan around the gap, for the role or skill you want.
The same 40 steps for everyone is why most roadmaps get abandoned.
How it works: 1 Tell Asta your goal — a role, a skill, a hobby, or "not sure yet". 2 Rate yourself. 3 Take a short assessment — a few questions per skill confirm your real level. 4 Get your plan — phases, milestones and resources for the gap only, sized to your hours and budget.
