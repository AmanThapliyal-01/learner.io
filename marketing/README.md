# learner.io brand assets

Creative direction, the asset set, how each piece was made, and how to regenerate it.

## Creative direction

**Idea.** The product's promise is "skip what you know, learn what you don't". Every asset draws
that as one picture: a path of stepping stones where the **solid** stones are what you already know,
the **hatched** stones are your gap (the same diagonal hatch as the app's `SkillGap` bars), and a
**sprout** (the brand mark) is the goal.

**Style.** Flat editorial illustration with thin ink outlines, soft rounded shapes and a faint
paper grain. Calm and low-contrast so it sits comfortably next to long reading sessions. No people,
no stock imagery, no text inside the art.

**Palette.** The app's own tokens (`web/src/styles/index.css`), nothing new:

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Sky / soft fill | `#dcefe8` primary-soft | `#19221f` |
| Ground | `#e9eeec` surface-muted | `#1f2a27` |
| Known stones, leaves | `#2a7360` primary | `#6cc3a8` |
| Gap hatch | `#2a7360` lines | `#6cc3a8` lines |
| Outlines | `#1b2623` ink | `#9cada8` ink-muted |
| Sun / glow (only warm note) | `#fbefd5` warning-soft | `#3a2e14` |

**Type.** Bricolage Grotesque 600 for display, Inter for everything else, as in the app.

## Tools, models and cost

| Tool | Used for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| OpenArt MCP, **Wan 2.7 Image** (standard, 2K) | 2 concept images: the stepping-stone landscape (16:9) and the sprout + chat bubble (4:3) | 6 credits each, **12 credits total** (40 → 28 left) |
| HyperFrames 0.8.172 (product-launch-video workflow) | Promo video, rendered locally | Free |
| HeyGen music library (via HyperFrames, signed in, free plan) | Background track | Library retrieval, not generation: no credit charge was reported. The CLI doesn't show a HeyGen credit balance, so this wasn't checked on the account itself. |
| Headless Chrome | OG card PNG | Free |

**Why the OpenArt images aren't shipped.** The account is on OpenArt's Free plan, and every Free
output carries a large "OpenArt" watermark across the middle. The two generations were used as
concept art; the shipped illustrations are SVGs redrawn from those compositions. That turned out
better for the app anyway: they're 3-5 KB, sharp at any size, and switch to dark mode. To ship raw
OpenArt output, upgrade the plan (https://openart.ai/pricing) and regenerate without a watermark.

**Why the video wasn't generated by OpenArt.** Every OpenArt video model costs 50-450 credits per
clip; the balance was 40.

## Regenerate

```bash
# OG card (Git Bash, from the repo root). Chrome needs absolute paths and a file:// URL.
"/c/Program Files/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe" --headless=new --hide-scrollbars --force-device-scale-factor=1 --window-size=1200,630 --virtual-time-budget=6000 --screenshot="$(pwd -W)/web/public/og-image.png" "file:///$(pwd -W)/marketing/og/og-image.html"
```

```bash
# Video: check, preview, render, then make the web copy
cd marketing/videos/learner-promo && npx hyperframes check && npx hyperframes render --quality high --output renders/learner-promo.mp4
```

```bash
ffmpeg -i marketing/videos/learner-promo/renders/learner-promo.mp4 -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 24 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -an web/public/assets/video/learner-promo.mp4
```

The video's frames live in `marketing/videos/learner-promo/compositions/frames/`; `STORYBOARD.md`
holds the plan and `BRIEF.md` the intent. `renders/` and `.hyperframes/` are git-ignored.

## Design review (apple-design)

Rating: **Good.** No Critical or High findings.

- **Contrast.** OG headline ink `#1b2623` on `#dcefe8` is 12.9:1; primary `#2a7360` on `#dcefe8` is
  4.7:1 at 58px display size (needs 3:1). The video's 78 text pairs all pass AA; the "40" in danger
  `#b4432f` on white is 5.6:1.
- **Dark mode.** Both SVGs ship a dark palette (`dark-mode.md` › "Make sure full-color images and
  icons look good in both appearances"). Checked on the landing page in both schemes.
- **Motion.** Typing dots honour reduced motion; landing reveals already go through the app's
  global reduced-motion rule (`accessibility.md` › Cognitive).
- **Branding.** The illustration lives in the content layer, not chrome, and the logo isn't
  repeated in the app (`branding.md` › "Ensure branding always defers to content").
- **Low:** the SVGs follow the *system* scheme. If the app ever adds an in-app theme switch
  (`data-theme`), an `<img>` can't see it; swap to inline SVG with token classes then.
- **Low:** `og:image` is relative. Most scrapers need an absolute URL; prefix the production origin
  once there is one.
- **Low:** video frames keep the bottom ~17% clear (HyperFrames' caption band), so frames 2-4 sit a
  little high when shown without captions.
