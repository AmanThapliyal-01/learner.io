# Idea

## I have written down the rough Idea of what I have in mind.

In Today era when 'AI' is advancing so quickly I am seeing that every content creator saying learn this, learn that some even gives the step that follow these steps to create that but none of them know the level of the candidates or the viewer so even if they follow those steps they might not get the desired result.

and so i thought of this what if there was a software whom I tell what profession I wanna purse or what skill or bunch of skills I want to learn and it creates a plan personalized of me based on what i know or what my current level is.

## this is the MVP I have in mind

* Take the general information about the user.
* User set their own target what Profession skill or independent skills they want to learn.
* An AI agent will work behind the scene and creates a plan based on the user current level of knowledge of those skills.
* First we will ask the user to rate themselves in each skills they already know after that based on users response we will take an assessment to guage their skills.
